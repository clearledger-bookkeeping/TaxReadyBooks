Refund Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Plain-language summary: bookkeeping work is a professional service. If you believe you were charged incorrectly, paid for work that was not delivered as agreed, or have another billing concern, contact us promptly so we can review it.

1. Service fees

Fees are based on the bookkeeping scope agreed with the client. Because bookkeeping work may involve time spent reviewing, categorising, reconciling, preparing reports, communicating, and correcting records, completed or substantially completed services are generally not refundable except where required by law or where TaxReady Books agrees otherwise in writing.

2. Cancellation

A client may request cancellation of ongoing bookkeeping services by contacting TaxReady Books. Any notice period, final work, outstanding fees, or handover obligations are governed by the client's service agreement or written arrangement.

3. Incorrect or duplicate charges

If you believe a payment was duplicated, incorrectly charged, or does not match the agreed arrangement, contact us with the relevant transaction details. We will investigate and, where an error is confirmed, correct it or issue an appropriate refund.

4. Undelivered or deficient service

If agreed bookkeeping work was not delivered or materially failed to match the agreed scope, contact us promptly. We may correct the work, complete the outstanding work, apply a credit, or issue a partial or full refund where appropriate and legally permitted.

5. Refund method and timing

Where a refund is approved, it will normally be returned using the original payment method where practical. Processing time can depend on the payment provider or bank.

6. Referral discounts

Referral discounts reduce the applicable bookkeeping service fee. They do not create a cash entitlement or refundable balance and cannot be exchanged for cash unless TaxReady Books expressly agrees otherwise in writing.

7. Taxes and statutory rights

Nothing in this policy is intended to limit a consumer right, refund right, cancellation right, or other protection that cannot lawfully be excluded under applicable law.

8. How to request a review

Email dipakkhadka.books@gmail.com with your name, the relevant service or invoice, date, and a short explanation. Do not email passwords or full payment-card details.
