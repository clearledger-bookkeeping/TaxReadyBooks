Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Our approach: TaxReady Books does not intentionally load optional third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking technologies until you give permission through the site's consent controls.

1. What cookies are

Cookies and similar technologies are small pieces of information stored by a browser or device. Some are necessary for a website to operate; others can be used for analytics, advertising, personalisation, or tracking.

2. Essential technologies

We may use strictly necessary browser storage or cookies for basic functions, security, preferences, and site operation. These are not used to create advertising profiles.

3. Third-party trackers

Optional third-party analytics, advertising pixels, social tracking, or similar technologies should be activated only after you select the relevant consent option. If you decline optional tracking, the site should keep those technologies disabled. If a third-party service changes its technology or privacy practices, this policy may be updated.

4. Your consent

The cookie controls are intended to give you a clear choice about optional third-party tracking. You can change or withdraw your optional tracking choice by using the cookie preferences control when available or by clearing site storage and revisiting the website. Withdrawing tracking consent does not withdraw consent you separately gave to receive emails.

5. Third-party services

Some website features may link to or rely on third-party providers. Their own cookies and privacy practices may apply when you leave the TaxReady Books website or interact with a provider-controlled service. Review the relevant provider's policy before submitting information there.

6. Contact

Questions about cookies or tracking can be sent to dipakkhadka.books@gmail.com.
