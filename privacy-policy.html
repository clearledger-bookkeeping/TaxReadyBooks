Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

TaxReady Books is based in Tulsipur, Nepal and provides QuickBooks Online bookkeeping services remotely. This policy explains how TaxReady Books handles personal information collected through this website and related communications.

Important: This website is designed to support compliance with applicable privacy requirements, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 where they apply. This page is not legal advice and does not replace a review by a qualified lawyer for your particular business, locations, clients, vendors, and technology stack.

1. Information we collect

  • Name and contact information such as email address and phone number when you contact us, request a review, make a referral, or otherwise provide it.
  • Business and bookkeeping information that you voluntarily provide when discussing or receiving bookkeeping services.
  • Information contained in documents you choose to send to us for bookkeeping purposes.
  • Website technical information that is necessary to operate, secure, and maintain the website.

Do not send passwords, payment-card numbers, authentication codes, or other information that is not reasonably necessary for your request through a public website form.

2. Why we use personal data

  • To respond to enquiries and provide requested services.
  • To arrange consultations and communicate about bookkeeping work.
  • To process referrals and apply the stated referral discounts.
  • To maintain records, prevent abuse, protect systems, and comply with legal obligations.
  • To send marketing or promotional emails only where you have separately agreed to receive them or where another lawful basis permits the communication.

3. Consent and your choices

Where consent is the basis for processing, we ask for consent for the specified purpose and provide a way to withdraw it. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that was already lawful before withdrawal or processing that may continue under another lawful basis permitted by law.

4. DPDP rights

Where the DPDP Act applies, you may have rights including access to information about your personal data, correction and updating, erasure subject to lawful retention requirements, grievance redressal, and nomination rights. We will provide a practical method to make requests using the contact information below. 5. Data sharing and service providers

We may use service providers for hosting, email, authentication, storage, forms, security, analytics, or other functions needed to operate the business. They may process data only as necessary for the service and subject to appropriate contractual or technical safeguards. We do not sell personal data.

6. Third-party trackers and cookies

TaxReady Books will not load optional third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking technologies unless you choose to allow them through the site's consent controls. Essential technical storage may be used where necessary to provide security or basic website functions. A cookie preference does not itself create consent for unrelated marketing emails.

7. Retention and security

We retain personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the stated purpose, legitimate business needs, legal obligations, dispute resolution, or security. We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. International processing

Because TaxReady Books serves clients remotely and may use service providers located in other countries, personal data may be processed outside Nepal or India where permitted by applicable law and subject to appropriate safeguards and provider terms. Where the DPDP Act applies, any government restrictions concerning transfers or availability of personal data will be followed.

9. Children

This website is intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly request personal data from children for marketing or service enquiries.
